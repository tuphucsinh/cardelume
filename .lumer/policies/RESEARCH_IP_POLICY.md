# Research & IP Policy

Market/competitor research is intelligence, never a source of derivative production templates. Preserve source URL, date and market; separate observation from inference.

Required creative flow: **reference → abstract insight → original CardeLume creative thesis**. Prohibited flow: competitor template → copied layout → changed colors/fonts.

Public availability does not establish a license. Pinterest, Google Images, Etsy and competitor assets are not production-safe by default. Unknown provenance/license means reject from production.
