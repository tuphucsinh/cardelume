## Required review for each family

Record 0–10 scores for Premium Art Direction, Originality, WOW, Emotional Resonance, Visual/Copy Harmony and Market/Language Naturalness. Also answer:

- Is the composition independently authored rather than recognizably derivative of a third-party card/template/artwork?
- Does it have one specific CardeLume signature move?
- Does it avoid generic AI/template aesthetics?
- Does it remain premium with short, medium and long copy?
- Does the design remain natural in EN, VI, JP, KR and ZH assignments?
- Does photo use, if available, feel intentional rather than mechanically inserted?
- Is there any near-duplicate family already in the launch portfolio?

Any uncertainty on originality, license/provenance, critical rendering or cultural appropriateness means **HOLD / DO NOT APPROVE**.
