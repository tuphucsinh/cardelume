## Owner attestation required

For each asset, record all of the following:

- creator / source;
- acquisition or creation date if known;
- whether CardeLume owns the asset or has a commercial license;
- whether commercial use, derivative use and redistribution/bundling are allowed as applicable;
- source/evidence file or URL;
- owner name and date;
- explicit statement that, to the owner's knowledge, the asset was not copied from a third-party logo/artwork without permission.

If any answer is unknown, keep the asset `UNKNOWN` and **DO NOT PUBLISH** it to production.
