## Current premium signals observed

### Paper Source / Smitten on Paper

Reference: https://www.papersource.com/pages/smitten-on-paper

Observed abstract signals:
- clean/timeless composition;
- selective foil/letterpress rather than decorative overload;
- premium paper/print cues as part of perceived value;
- restrained modern + vintage tension.

Allowed CardeLume abstraction: use negative space, one tactile metaphor and strong type hierarchy. Do **not** reconstruct any Smitten layout, artwork, border or ornament.

### Minted / letterpress personalized stationery

Reference: https://www.minted.com/product/personalized-stationery-thank-you-cards/MIN-015-LPY/delicate

Observed abstract signals:
- personalization can be the focal point;
- premium cotton/letterpress increases perceived keepsake value;
- minimalism benefits from texture and proportion, not extra decoration.

Allowed CardeLume abstraction: Monogram Orbit / Quiet Seal / Museum Note may make recipient metadata itself the artwork. Do **not** use Minted typography/layout as a reconstruction target.

### Minted / editorial single-photo direction

Reference: https://www.minted.com/product/save-the-date-cards/MIN-72G-STD/editorial-lettering

Observed abstract signals:
- one photo can carry most of the visual emotion;
- simple editorial lettering and disciplined margins protect the photo;
- premium does not require ornamental density.

Allowed CardeLume abstraction: Memory Window uses an original asymmetric photo fragment + micro-caption system, not the referenced layout.

### Paper Source / personalized correspondence

Reference: https://www.papersource.com/collections/personalized-stationery

Observed abstract signals:
- botanical, vintage and minimalist directions coexist, but typography/format choices are core customization primitives;
- customer choice can stay bounded without exposing a Canva-like editor.

Allowed CardeLume abstraction: keep controlled template families and AI-directed variation; do not import marketplace template inventory.

## Research-to-template checklist

Before implementing a concept inspired by market research, record:
- URLs consulted;
- abstract observations only;
- an original CardeLume thesis written without visual reconstruction instructions;
- original procedural primitives/assets;
- similarity review against the references;
- provenance/IP approval before staging/production.
