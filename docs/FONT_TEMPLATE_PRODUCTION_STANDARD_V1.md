# CardeLume Font & Template Production Standard v1

## Decision

CardeLume does not equate **free/open-source** with **production-ready**, and does not equate **repo-authored template code** with **premium/originality-approved**.

### Font production-ready means all of the following

1. authoritative license evidence;
2. commercial design use allowed;
3. embedding/bundling terms known;
4. exact artifact/version pinned;
5. shipped binary SHA256 recorded;
6. license/copyright evidence retained;
7. assigned locale glyph coverage verified;
8. browser/final-render typography QA and metric parity pass;
9. no uncontrolled fallback for a supported locale.

OFL family eligibility is therefore a prerequisite, not final approval.

### Template production-ready means all of the following

1. immutable renderer-supported template/version pair;
2. every font/asset approved;
3. no unknown external artwork/template dependency;
4. originality attestation and third-party visual-similarity review complete;
5. short/medium/long copy pressure and assigned-locale typography QA pass;
6. preview/final parity and clipping/contrast checks pass;
7. Golden benchmark target-segment median >= 8.5 and p10 >= 7.5;
8. human Premium Art Direction, Originality and WOW review passes;
9. no portfolio near-duplicate/concentration warning.

## Typography portfolio rule

Do not turn CardeLume into a font catalog. The desired launch system is intentionally small:

- one primary global UI sans;
- one primary Latin premium display serif;
- a small renderer-safe Latin body/display set;
- explicit locale families for JP/KR/ZH;
- experimental fonts remain behind flags until benchmark evidence justifies promotion.

## New commercial-safe experiment candidates verified 2026-08-28

- Be Vietnam Pro — VI-first UI/body alternate;
- Lora — warm/heartfelt serif;
- Spectral — premium editorial/long-copy serif;
- Source Serif 4 — renderer/editorial robustness candidate;
- Literata — literary/intimate serif;
- Crimson Pro — soft classic premium serif.

All are listed by Google Fonts metadata as OFL and include Vietnamese. They remain experiment-only until exact binary evidence and CardeLume typography benchmarks pass.

## Current template portfolio decision

The 16 legacy seeds are structurally IP-safer than marketplace templates because the renderer uses repo-authored procedural SVG/code and no external template image dependency was found. They are **not all launch-quality**.

Core/refine: Luxury Editorial, Midnight Lume, Photo Story, Quiet Minimal, Classic Letterpress.

Niche/rework: Botanical Poise, Art Deco Noir.

Hold/reframe: Washi Elegance, Soft Seoul.

Merge/rework: Quiet Noir, Celestial Night.

Replace/major rework: Watercolor Bloom, Golden Hour, Bold Pop, Kawaii Joy, Little Wonders.

The Step 17C original experiment families are preferred as replacement directions because they use original theses and procedural primitives rather than downloaded template artwork.
