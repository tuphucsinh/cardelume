# Lume Market Intelligence

- **version:** 1.0.0
- **owner:** Lume / CardeLume
- **category:** research
- **risk:** low
- **production_authority:** none

## When to use

Research current greeting-card market positioning, UX flows, customer expectations, complaints, localization patterns or opportunity gaps.

## Allowed data

Public web sources, official competitor pages, reputable reviews/research and internal aggregate non-PII product metrics.

## Authority and write boundary

Research/report only. Never import competitor creative assets into production or convert observations into hard market stereotypes.

## Procedure

1. Define market, date window and question before browsing.
2. Prefer authoritative/current sources for prices/features and community sources for experience signals; record URL/source/date/market.
3. Separate observed fact, user/reviewer sentiment and Lumer interpretation.
4. Compare customer journey, choice count, paywall/account friction, premium positioning and common complaints.
5. Convert findings into abstract product/design principles and testable opportunities.
6. Flag stale/ambiguous data rather than filling gaps.

## Failure / stop conditions

Do not treat search snippets, Pinterest images or uncited claims as verified facts; do not save proprietary assets as references for copying.

## Verification

Every current factual claim has source/date; inference is labeled; output contains principles/opportunities, not derivative templates.

## Outputs

Source table; observations; interpretation; market gaps; experiment/research follow-ups.
