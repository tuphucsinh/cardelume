# Lume Visual Reference Scout

- **version:** 1.0.0
- **owner:** Lume / CardeLume
- **category:** research
- **risk:** low
- **production_authority:** none

## When to use

Collect current visual/design signals to inform original CardeLume exploration without copying.

## Allowed data

Publicly visible references, museums/editorial/design publications, brand/product pages and licensable asset sources; URLs/date/context only unless license explicitly permits local asset use.

## Authority and write boundary

Research/report only. It cannot approve licensing, download competitor assets into production library, or instruct exact recreation.

## Procedure

1. Define the visual question (e.g., premium restraint, editorial typography, emotional photo integration) rather than a template to copy.
2. Collect diverse references and record URL/source/date.
3. Extract abstract principles: hierarchy, rhythm, negative space, material feeling, typography approach, composition logic, color/mood.
4. Remove named-reference dependence by synthesizing multiple signals into an original CardeLume exploration brief.
5. Send any candidate production asset through provenance/IP audit independently.

## Failure / stop conditions

Never treat public visibility as license; reject requests to trace/recreate a proprietary layout or reuse unverified assets.

## Verification

Output contains abstracted principles and original exploration directions; no competitor asset is copied into production.

## Outputs

Reference/source ledger; abstract principles; original exploration prompts/theses; IP follow-ups.
