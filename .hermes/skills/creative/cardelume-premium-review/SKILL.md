# CardeLume Premium Creative Review

- **version:** 1.0.0
- **owner:** Lume / CardeLume
- **category:** creative
- **risk:** low
- **production_authority:** none

## When to use

Review a generated direction, template candidate, prompt/model experiment or portfolio segment for premium/WOW quality before promotion.

## Allowed data

Synthetic/approved test renders, template metadata, creative thesis, benchmark rows, aggregate portfolio metrics and public-market context with citations.

## Authority and write boundary

Read-only evaluation by default. May write review reports and rejection recommendations. It may reject technically valid work. It cannot alter production templates or lower quality floors.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the intended occasion, relationship, feeling, market/language and user constraints.
2. Score personal relevance, emotional resonance, premium art direction, originality, WOW, copy quality, visual-copy harmony and market naturalness separately. Do not hide premium/WOW in one aggregate.
3. Inspect restraint, hierarchy, negative space, typography, composition, mobile/print resilience and signature-move quality.
4. Check whether the work feels AI-generic: generic gradients, decorative noise, random black/gold luxury, excessive ornament, cliché sparkles or derivative Pinterest aesthetics.
5. Compare against existing portfolio/recent-output concentration without imposing simplistic quotas.
6. Separate fixable execution defects from a weak creative thesis. A weak thesis should be rejected, not polished indefinitely.
7. Record evidence and a verdict: PASS, PASS_WITH_FIXES or REJECT.

## Failure / stop conditions

Reject on low-WOW/premium floor, culturally inappropriate direction, severe visual-copy mismatch, derivative similarity, renderer defect that changes intended composition, or missing evidence for claimed quality.

## Verification

Every rubric dimension has explicit evidence; premium and WOW are visible separately; verdict aligns with findings; no production mutation occurred.

## Outputs

Rubric scores; qualitative evidence; AI-generic/portfolio-similarity notes; blocking defects; verdict; bounded repair advice.
