# CardeLume Market Adaptation

- **version:** 1.0.0
- **owner:** Lume / CardeLume
- **category:** creative
- **risk:** medium
- **production_authority:** approval-required

## When to use

Adapt a CardeLume template/copy behavior for a market or locale without creating stereotypes or breaking global design DNA.

## Allowed data

Approved template source, locale/script requirements, native-language QA notes, current public research, synthetic briefs and approved font manifests.

## Authority and write boundary

May prepare locale variants in experiment/staging. Cannot turn market affinity into a hard creative rule except objective compatibility/exclusion; production promotion requires owner-approved release.

## Procedure

1. Define objective adaptation needs: script support, typography metrics, line breaking, culturally sensitive symbols, copy conventions and format expectations.
2. Separate hard technical compatibility from soft market affinity.
3. Preserve the original family thesis while adjusting bounded typography/spacing/copy or approved controlled variants.
4. Stress Latin/CJK/Hangul as applicable, including long recipient names and difficult punctuation/line-break cases.
5. Use native-language review where launch-critical; distinguish machine inference from verified language feedback.
6. Benchmark adapted directions and compare against the non-adapted baseline.
7. Document why each change is technical/cultural evidence rather than stereotype.

## Failure / stop conditions

Stop on unverified cultural claim used as a hard rule, unapproved font, clipping/overflow, or adaptation that materially copies a local competitor.

## Verification

Script/render compatibility passes; market affinity remains soft; native-language QA status is explicit; benchmark delta recorded.

## Outputs

Adaptation brief; changed controlled parameters; typography matrix; language QA status; benchmark comparison; promotion recommendation.
