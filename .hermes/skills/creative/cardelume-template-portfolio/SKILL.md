# CardeLume Template Portfolio Manager

- **version:** 1.0.0
- **owner:** Lume / CardeLume
- **category:** creative
- **risk:** low
- **production_authority:** approval-required

## When to use

Assess portfolio health, exposure, creative concentration, market gaps, freshness and candidates to improve/archive.

## Allowed data

Template catalog metadata, exposure-normalized impressions/selections/paid outcomes, critic/expand/fallback association, benchmark concentration and aggregate market metrics.

## Authority and write boundary

May analyze and propose experiment/staging changes. May not archive, activate, promote or materially reweight production catalog behavior without the normal release/owner approval path.

## Procedure

1. Normalize outcomes by exposure and segment by family, visual archetype, locale/market and age.
2. Report top-family/top-3 share, visual archetype concentration, accent/palette concentration and repeated-thesis patterns.
3. Cross-tab template families with critic, expand and fallback rates to detect hidden creative weakness.
4. Identify underserved occasions/scripts/photo modes and underexposed high-editorial new work.
5. Distinguish catalog quality from ranker exposure bias; never optimize raw usage alone.
6. Recommend keep/improve/experiment/archive candidates with evidence and expected portfolio effect.
7. Route any production catalog mutation through publish/release approval.

## Failure / stop conditions

Stop if metrics are content-sensitive/customer-identifying, exposure data are missing for conversion comparisons, or recommendation would encode stereotypes as hard rules.

## Verification

Uses normalized metrics; reports concentration separately from conversion; shows uncertainty/gaps; production changes are only proposed.

## Outputs

Portfolio scorecard; concentration table; gap map; improve/archive candidates; experiment recommendations.
